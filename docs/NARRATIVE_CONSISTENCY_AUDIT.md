# Narrative Consistency Audit

**Build audited:** Complete World Experiment 1  
**Audit date:** 2026-08-11  
**Status rule:** This playable build is an explicitly proposed branch. It does not promote experimental god-death methods, minor-god fates, regional incidents, tulip outcomes, or new characters into approved canon.

## Result

No unresolved canon conflict or player-facing contradiction remains in the experimental build after correction. The build preserves canonical map facts, identities, relationships, and knowledge boundaries while labeling all new connective material as experimental.

## Canon preserved

- The protagonist is the Hero's Moon-favored brother. He disobeyed the campaign order, reached the Northern Academy too late, withdrew to Stalwart, and begins from survival rather than destiny.
- The Hero was Sun-favored, killed the Demon King at The Fear, and receives the protagonist's journey memories only when their connected souls meet there.
- The Demon King was the Record Keeper. He followed a different kind human than The Unbroken did, mistook emotion for a possession, and became addicted to unstable stolen fragments.
- The first witnessed murder exposes only the Record Keeper's false scarcity justification. It does not expose the council purge.
- Only Sizzling/Memory and Measure proves the all-demon council's authorized witness purge and engineered disaster. Only Boiling/Life and Provision proves supplies were sufficient. The Truth memory requires preserving both archives, not merely reading one.
- All four ancient cities are equal hearts of the mixed empire: Memory and Measure, Life and Provision, Works and Protection, and Possibility and Exchange.
- The four-city disaster propagated through the synchronized imperial system and exceeded the council's intended one-or-two-city sacrifice.
- The first recorded battle remains an unmarked site in Demon territory near the future Central and Desert borders. The experiment does not invent a coordinate or later name.
- The Hunt remains Demon territory at `(1,9)`, close to the Northern border.
- The Unbroken never witnessed the purge. He cannot reveal it. His species becomes inferable through five regional clues and confirmable only through a late meeting enabled by Teren-Seven's trust.
- No known Moonmaiden is a demon. Moonmaiden remains gender-neutral in honor of the First Moonmaiden. The protagonist's Moon favor does not establish that he was one.
- Divine power, world-magic, and the Demon King's stolen-emotion power remain separate.
- The Moon is permanently dead. The Old Sword recovers through the protagonist's human soul carrying Moon-shaped values, not through resurrection or hidden divine power.
- The tulip is the world's request, not a morality reward. The world's love is constant, mana remains separately growable, and finding the flower opens a possibility rather than completing a cure.

## Intentional ambiguity retained

- The exact canonical deaths of the three principal gods and the fates of the wider pantheon remain undecided outside this experiment.
- The First Moonmaiden's miracle, the Last Standard-Bearer's identity and chronology, and several shrine accounts remain disputed.
- The cause of the world's love remains unknown and is never treated as proof of virtue.
- The exact first battlefield coordinate, The Unbroken's original name and lifespan mechanism, and the Peeping God's present state remain unresolved.
- The player may end with restoration, localized renewal, a shared beginning, uncertain onward travel, partial release, two ghosts, or a moonless connected world. These are experimental outcomes, not approved ending canon.

## Narrative spine verified

Mera's route book gives the protagonist a reason to leave that is larger than scavenging but smaller than destiny. The regions then supply six experiences—Care, Duty, Limitation, Trust, Truth, and Future. These experiences change the sword in visible stages and give the Hero new memories only at The Fear. A full journey lets the brothers understand that care is not invalidated by failure, an ending does not waste prior sacrifice, and love does not require identical deaths. The tulip decision follows the brother's passing so world-restoration never replaces the personal resolution.
